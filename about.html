Our Legacy

A Journey of Generations.

From a single grocery shop in Kanak Mandi to J&K's No.1 Pulses Supplier — the same values, six decades on.

Since 1960

It all began with a small grocery shop in the heart of Kanak Mandi — and a simple belief.

Do good business, keep your word, and earn trust that lasts. That belief is where the Durga Sales story starts, and it's the same belief that still runs the business today.

From humble part loads to full truckloads, and from trucks to big containers, the journey has grown with time. But behind every milestone has been the same hard work, the same honesty, and the same relationships built over generations.

What started as a shop slowly became a name. What started as a business became a legacy. And what changed with time was only the scale — never the values.

Our Growth

From a Mandi Shop to J&K's No.1 Pulses Supplier

1960

Durga Sales opens as a small grocery shop in Kanak Mandi, Jammu — built on honesty and keeping every word given.

Early Years

Business grows from humble part loads to full truckloads, as relationships with retailers across the region are built one order at a time.

Expansion

From trucks to big containers — Nav Durga Sales opens with a dedicated warehouse at 65-C & 66-C, Jammu, to serve growing demand across Jammu & Kashmir.

Today

Durga Sales Group stands as J&K's No.1 Pulses Supplier — importers & traders of premium rajma, urad, chana, kabuli chana, toor, besan and lobia, trusted by retailers and businesses across the state, and an authorised superstockist of Rajdhani Besan.
