Freshness

What fresh means at a dal shop.

  1. Stock doesn't sit.

    Besan and pulses come in on several trucks through the week, so what goes out is latest-date stock, not last season's.

  2. Summer gets stricter.

    Heat and damp are hard on dal and besan. In the summer months we restock almost every alternate day.

  3. Cleaned and sorted.

    Even grains cook evenly. It is the first thing a halwai or a hotel kitchen notices, and the reason they come back.

  4. Brands you can check.

    Our own DS Diamond and DS Pulses packs, Rajdhani Besan as authorised superstockist, and Rajat Delhi kabuli chana, which you will only find with us.
